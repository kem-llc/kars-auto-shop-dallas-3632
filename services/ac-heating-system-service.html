AC & Heating System Service

Keep your cabin comfortable with AC recharge, compressor repair, and heater service.

Schedule this service

Overview

A malfunctioning AC or heater can make driving miserable. Our certified technicians diagnose and repair all climate control systems. We perform refrigerant recharges, compressor and condenser replacements, blower motor repairs, and heater core services. We use OEM-spec refrigerants and components to restore comfort quickly.

What's included

  • Consistent cabin temperature year-round
  • Improved air quality with cabin filter replacement
  • Prevent compressor failure with early service
  • Backed by warranty on parts and labor

How it works

1

System Diagnosis

We test pressure, temperature, and electrical components to identify the root cause of the issue.

2

Repair or Recharge

We perform the necessary repairs or recharge the system to manufacturer specifications.

3

Performance Verification

After repair, we run the system to ensure proper cooling or heating and check for leaks.

Common questions

How do I know if my AC needs a recharge?

If the air isn't as cold as before or takes longer to cool, it may need a recharge. We can check for leaks and top it off.

Do you repair heaters too?

Absolutely. We service both AC and heating systems, including heater cores, blowers, and thermostats.
