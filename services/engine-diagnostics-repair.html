Engine Diagnostics & Repair

Accurate diagnostics and expert repairs for check engine lights, performance issues, and more.

Schedule this service

Overview

When your check engine light comes on or your car isn't performing as it should, trust Kars Auto Shop to diagnose the problem accurately. Our advanced scanners and experienced technicians identify the root cause quickly. We then provide a clear explanation and repair options, using quality parts to restore your engine's performance and reliability.

What's included

  • Restore engine power and fuel efficiency
  • Prevent costly breakdowns with early detection
  • Extend the life of your vehicle
  • Backed by a comprehensive warranty

How it works

1

Computer Scan & Inspection

We connect your vehicle to our diagnostic system to read error codes and perform a thorough visual inspection.

2

Detailed Report & Estimate

You receive a plain-language explanation of the issue, along with a transparent estimate for the recommended repairs.

3

Professional Repair & Testing

Our certified technicians perform the repair using quality parts, then test-drive your car to ensure everything is working perfectly.
