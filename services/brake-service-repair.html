Common questions

How often should I replace my brake pads?

Typically every 30,000-50,000 miles, but it depends on driving habits. We inspect them at every visit.

Can you fix a soft brake pedal?

Yes, soft pedals often indicate air in the lines or a fluid leak. We'll diagnose and repair the issue.
