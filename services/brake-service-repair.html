Brake Service & Repair

Complete brake inspections, pad replacement, rotor resurfacing, and fluid flushes for safe stopping.

Schedule this service

Overview

Your brakes are your vehicle's most critical safety system. At Kars Auto Shop, we offer comprehensive brake services—from pad and rotor replacement to caliper repair and brake fluid flushes. We use high-quality components to ensure reliable stopping power and quiet operation. Our thorough inspection catches wear early, saving you money and keeping you safe.

What's included

  • Shorter stopping distances
  • Eliminate squeaking and vibration
  • Extend brake component life
  • Peace of mind on every drive

How it works

1

Brake System Inspection

We check pads, rotors, calipers, lines, and fluid level to assess overall brake health.

2

Recommendation & Approval

We show you the condition of your brakes and explain what needs to be replaced or serviced.

3

Precision Installation & Test

Our technicians install quality parts, bleed the system if needed, and test brakes for optimal performance.

Common questions

How often should I replace my brake pads?

Typically every 30,000 to 60,000 miles, but it depends on driving habits. We inspect them at every service.

Do you offer brake pad warranties?

Yes, we warranty all brake parts and labor. Ask us for details during your visit.
