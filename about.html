Our story

Dallas' Trusted Auto Repair Since 2003

To provide honest, high-quality auto repair services that keep our community safe on the road.

Kars Auto Shop was founded in 2003 by master mechanic Carlos M. with a simple vision: create a repair shop where customers are treated like family. Starting with a small two-bay garage on Kline Drive, Carlos built a reputation for integrity and technical skill that quickly spread throughout Dallas. Over the years, we've grown to a full-service facility with six bays and a team of ASE-certified technicians, but our core values remain unchanged.

We invest in the latest diagnostic technology and continuous training so that we can service modern vehicles with confidence. From the first handshake to the final test drive, we prioritize clear communication and quality workmanship. Every car that leaves our shop is a testament to our commitment to excellence.
