Our story

Your Trusted Dallas Auto Repair Partner

To provide honest, high-quality auto repair that keeps our community safe and mobile. We believe in transparency, craftsmanship, and treating every customer like a neighbor.

Kars Auto Shop was founded in 2008 by a team of ASE-certified mechanics who saw a need for trustworthy auto repair in Dallas. Tired of upselling and shoddy work, they set out to build a shop where customers always come first. Starting with just two bays, the shop quickly earned a reputation for honest diagnostics and fair pricing—word spread across the city.

Today, Kars Auto Shop serves thousands of Dallas drivers from our full-service facility on Kline Drive. Our team has grown, but our values haven't changed. We invest in the latest tools, continuous training, and a comfortable waiting area because we believe a great experience goes hand-in-hand with great repairs. Whether it's a routine oil change or a complex engine rebuild, we treat every car as if it were our own.
